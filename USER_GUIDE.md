# User Guide - Cattle Disease AI

## Introduction

Cattle Disease AI is a mobile application that helps farmers and veterinarians diagnose cattle diseases early using artificial intelligence. The app works completely offline, protecting your privacy and allowing use in remote areas without internet.

## Features

✅ **Offline Disease Detection** - AI runs on your device  
✅ **Easy Image Capture** - Use camera or select from gallery  
✅ **Detailed Results** - Get disease name, confidence score, symptoms, and recommendations  
✅ **History Tracking** - Keep records of all diagnoses  
✅ **No Internet Required** - Works anywhere, anytime  
✅ **Privacy First** - All data stays on your device  

## Getting Started

### Installation

1. Download the app from the release page or build from source
2. Install on your Android or iOS device
3. Grant camera and storage permissions when prompted
4. You're ready to start!

## How to Use

### 1. Home Screen

When you open the app, you'll see two main options:
- **New Diagnosis**: Start a new disease detection
- **Diagnosis History**: View past diagnoses

### 2. Making a New Diagnosis

#### Step 1: Capture Image
1. Tap "New Diagnosis"
2. Tap "Capture Image"
3. Choose:
   - **Camera**: Take a new photo
   - **Gallery**: Select existing photo

**Tips for Best Results:**
- Take clear, well-lit photos
- Focus on the affected area
- Avoid blurry images
- Get close enough to see details
- Natural lighting works best

#### Step 2: Run Analysis
1. After selecting image, tap "Run Diagnosis"
2. Wait a few seconds for processing
3. Results will appear automatically

#### Step 3: Review Results

The app will show:
- **Disease Name**: What disease was detected
- **Confidence Score**: How certain the AI is (percentage)
- **Explanation**: What this disease is
- **Symptoms**: Common signs to look for
- **Recommendations**: What actions to take

**Understanding Confidence Scores:**
- 🟢 **70-100%**: High confidence - likely accurate
- 🟡 **Below 70%**: Lower confidence - consult a vet

#### Step 4: Save to History
1. Tap the save icon (💾) in the top-right
2. Diagnosis is saved to your history
3. You can access it anytime

### 3. Viewing History

1. From home screen, tap "Diagnosis History"
2. See all your past diagnoses
3. Each entry shows:
   - Thumbnail image
   - Disease name
   - Confidence score
   - When it was diagnosed

#### Managing History
- **View Details**: Tap any diagnosis to see full details
- **Delete**: Tap the trash icon to remove a diagnosis
- **Refresh**: Tap refresh icon to reload list

### 4. Viewing Diagnosis Details

Tap any diagnosis in history to see:
- Full-size image
- Complete diagnosis information
- Timestamp of when diagnosed
- All symptoms and recommendations

## Supported Diseases

The app can detect these cattle diseases:

### 1. Healthy
Normal, healthy cattle with no disease signs.

### 2. Foot and Mouth Disease (FMD)
- **Symptoms**: Blisters, excessive salivation, lameness, fever
- **Action**: Isolate immediately, contact vet urgently

### 3. Lumpy Skin Disease (LSD)
- **Symptoms**: Skin nodules, fever, reduced milk production
- **Action**: Vaccinate herd, separate affected animals

### 4. Mastitis
- **Symptoms**: Swollen udder, abnormal milk, reduced yield
- **Action**: Milk out affected quarters, antibiotics, improve hygiene

### 5. Pink Eye
- **Symptoms**: Watery eyes, redness, eye discharge, squinting
- **Action**: Isolate, apply eye treatment, reduce dust/flies

## Troubleshooting

### Camera Not Working
- Check app has camera permission
- Try restarting the app
- Ensure camera works in other apps

### Can't Select Images
- Check storage permission
- Ensure images exist in gallery
- Try taking new photo

### Results Seem Wrong
- Ensure good image quality
- Check confidence score
- Retake image with better lighting
- Consult veterinarian for confirmation

### App Crashes
- Restart the app
- Clear app cache in settings
- Reinstall if problem persists

### History Empty
- Ensure you saved diagnoses
- Try refresh button
- Check you haven't deleted items
